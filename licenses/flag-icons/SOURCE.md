# flag-icons

Country flag SVGs from [lipis/flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/tree/v7.3.2), pinned to v7.3.2.

The bundled US, Brazil, France, Spain, Germany, Saudi Arabia, Israel, China, Japan, and Palestine assets are unmodified files from `flags/4x3/`. They replace the simplified hand-painted flags. MIT license: see [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt).
